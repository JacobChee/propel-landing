Best Property Marketing Tools for Singapore Agents (2026)

Updated June 2026 · By District10

Marketing a property listing used to mean hiring a photographer, waiting 3 days, and posting on PropertyGuru. In 2026, agents who close faster use video, virtual staging, WhatsApp broadcasts, and social content together — and they use tools to produce it without breaking the marketing budget.

This is an honest guide to the best options available to Singapore property agents. We've included our own service (District10) alongside competitors — because giving you the full picture is more useful than a one-sided pitch.

Listing Video Tools

Listings with video receive more enquiries than photo-only listings. Video matters most for condos above S$1.5M, Instagram Reels, and WhatsApp broadcasts where a short clip catches attention before the viewer reads anything.

PostAI DIY Tool

Pastes a PropertyGuru or 99.co URL and generates a branded video with AI voiceover, captions, and music in under 60 seconds. Serves 1,200+ Singapore agents. Bilingual English and Chinese support. Best for high-volume agents who want speed and control.

What it doesn't cover: Virtual staging, WhatsApp scripts, open house flyers.

Price: S$99/month (60 credits) or S$1,088/year Turnaround: Under 60 seconds (DIY)
District10 Done-For-You

Done-for-you listing video produced by District10's team using AI-assisted production and human review. Delivered as part of a full listing kit alongside virtual staging, Instagram post, and WhatsApp script. Better suited for agents who want quality output without doing the work themselves.

Disclosure: This is our own service. We're including it because it's relevant — but we'd encourage you to compare.

Price: From S$149/listing (full kit). First kit free. Turnaround: 4 hours

Virtual Staging Services

Virtual staging shows buyers what a room could look like furnished — without the S$2,000–6,000 cost of physical staging. For most HDB and condo listings, virtual staging is the better ROI choice.

BoxBrownie Global Service

The most established global virtual staging brand. Upload your empty room photo, choose a style, and receive a staged image within 24–48 hours. Quality is consistent. No Singapore-specific features.

What it doesn't cover: Listing video, social posts, WhatsApp scripts.

Price: US$30 (≈S$40) per image Turnaround: 24–48 hours
VirtualStaging.sg Singapore-Based

Singapore-focused virtual staging service offering 7 staged images for S$45 with three interior style options. Good value for standalone staging needs. Slower than same-day options at 24–48 hours turnaround.

What it doesn't cover: Listing video, social posts, WhatsApp scripts.

Price: S$45 per listing (7 images) Turnaround: 24–48 hours

Our Recommendation by Agent Type

High-volume agent (6+ listings/month)

PostAI for video production efficiency, VirtualStaging.sg or BoxBrownie for standalone staging, and DIY social posts using Canva. Budget for a monthly retainer with a full-service agency if you want to reclaim more hours.

Active agent (2–5 listings/month)

A full-service option like District10 per listing typically works out more cost-effective than separate subscriptions, and saves 3–4 hours of production time per listing. First kit is free — worth testing before committing.

New agent or occasional listings

Start with virtual staging (BoxBrownie or VirtualStaging.sg) for photos and use free tools like Canva for basic social posts. Add video and WhatsApp scripts when you have active listings where they'll make a difference.
