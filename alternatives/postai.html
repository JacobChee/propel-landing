Where PostAI Shines

PostAI is genuinely impressive for what it does. The ability to paste a PropertyGuru URL and get a branded video in under 60 seconds is real and useful. For high-volume agents who have 10+ listings a month and just need fast video content for social media, the S$99/month model can make sense.

PostAI also offers bilingual (English and Chinese) voiceovers and captions — a meaningful advantage for agents serving both English and Mandarin-speaking clients.

The Core Limitation: You're Still Doing the Work

PostAI is a tool, not a service. After generating your video, you still need to review it, customise it if it doesn't look right, write your own WhatsApp broadcast, design your open house flyer, and decide on your staging approach. For busy agents, that's still 1–2 hours of production work per listing.

The AI-generated videos also pull directly from listing photos — which means if your photos are average, your video is average. District10 applies cinematic editing and production judgment on top of the raw material.
