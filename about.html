Where we started atsell.io · Marketing

We started in marketing. We never left.

Before District10, we built atsell.io — an ecommerce marketing agency. That work taught us what actually makes marketing perform: clear positioning, content that converts, and the discipline to test everything before you scale it.

We're not a tech company that decided to do marketing. We're marketers who got very good at using technology. Property is a new vertical for us — but knowing how to make content that drives action is not.

What changed 2023–2024 · The AI shift

AI changed what's possible. Most agents are being left behind.

AI didn't just make things faster — it fundamentally changed the cost structure of high-quality marketing. What used to require a videographer, a staging company, a social media manager, and a copywriter can now be done by a small team with the right tools.

But most agents are either ignoring it entirely, or being sold bad AI — generic captions, robotic videos, digital avatars that undermine the trust they've spent years building.

We saw the gap. Good operators who deserved better marketing, being failed by both traditional vendors (too slow, too expensive) and AI tools (too generic, too risky to put your name on).

What makes us different The thing we do first

We test everything before we use it.

We've evaluated over 40 AI tools across video generation, image staging, copywriting, and ad creative. Most of them aren't ready for client work. Some of them are. We only use the ones that clear our bar — and that bar is simple: would we put our name on this?

Every tool we use on your listings, we've tested on our own. Every output that leaves our desk has been reviewed by a human who understands property marketing. That's not a process we're building — it's how we've operated from day one.

We are at the forefront of AI marketing technology because we invest in staying there. But being first doesn't mean being reckless. It means you get access to what actually works, not what's just new.

How we work

Three things we won't compromise on.

These aren't values on a wall. They're how we make decisions when it's easier not to.

01

Human review on every deliverable

AI handles production speed. We handle quality. Every video, staging image, caption, and script is reviewed before it reaches you — not because we don't trust the tools, but because your name goes on it, not ours. If it's not good enough to close a deal, it doesn't leave our desk.

02

Property is a long-term trust business

Clients choose agents because they trust them. Nothing we produce should undermine that trust — not now, not three years from now when a potential client searches your name. Content that builds short-term reach but damages long-term credibility isn't marketing. It's a liability. We won't produce it.

03

We test before we sell

No tool, workflow, or output format goes near a client listing until it's cleared our internal bar. We run every new AI capability through our own work first. If we wouldn't use it ourselves, we don't offer it. Being at the forefront means knowing what to adopt — and what to leave behind.

Where we draw the line

What we won't do.

Being AI-first means making deliberate choices about where AI belongs — and where it doesn't.

✕

No AI-generated agent avatars or digital personas

Property transactions run on trust. An agent's face and name carry years of relationship capital. We will not produce fake digital versions of you, regardless of how convincing the technology gets. Your identity is not a marketing asset we get to automate.

✕

No templated content that makes you look like every other agent

The point of marketing is differentiation. Every deliverable is produced for your specific listing, your specific buyer profile, your specific voice — not pulled from a template library and swapped with your name at the top.

✕

No content that hasn't been reviewed for CEA compliance

Your licence is not worth a faster turnaround. Every piece of content we produce is checked against CEA guidelines before it leaves our desk. No exceptions, no shortcuts.

✕

No using a tool on clients that we haven't tested ourselves

We don't beta test on your listings. If a new AI tool or workflow hasn't cleared our internal quality bar, it doesn't get used on client work. Period. You're not paying to be an experiment.
